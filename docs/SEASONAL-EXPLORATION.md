# Seasonal exploration

The month selector applies to the same dates in every selected year. A January–May selection, for example, uses those five months in each year of the chosen period. A range that crosses December includes both ends. At least one month must remain selected.

The climate service evaluates the existing daily comfort rules only on selected dates. Coverage must be at least 90% in each selected month of each year. The score is the mean of the eligible daily scores; comfortable days are scaled to the selected calendar days per year, so a full-coverage January–May selection has at most 151 or 152 days in a single year. Sunshine is a daily average over the same dates. Missing AQI retains the existing weather-only treatment. AQI counts and summaries use the same selected dates; the monthly plot and table show selected months only.

Population, urban-centre density, homicide and regional budget are independent city-context datasets. Month selection does not change their source year or geographic scope. The map and ranking share the selected months, while their display regions use the same climate grid. East & Southeast Asia and Latin America use named-country sets for city ranking and rectangular extents for the map view; the visible map bounds can include neighboring countries.
